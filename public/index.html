How to read it

  • Reported within 15 s
  • 15–60 s old
  • Over a minute old
  • Position frozen — too old to extrapolate
  • Multilaterated, less precise than ADS-B
  • On the ground

Aircraft move between updates by dead reckoning from their last reported heading and speed. After 30 seconds without a fix the estimate stops and the symbol is marked, because past that it would be a guess.

Replay. OpenSky refuses connections from data centres, so a deployed page cannot read it live. This is a recording of real traffic, played back at the speed it happened — every age shown is the age that position actually had. Run it locally for live data.

Coastline and lakes traced from OpenStreetMap, © its contributors, ODbL. Simplified and shipped with the page — no tile server, no map library.
